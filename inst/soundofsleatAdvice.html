Sound of Sleat Summary Sheet

The Sound of Sleat (Trial Area 5) is the narrow strait separating the Isle of Skye from the Scottish mainland. Known for its deep, clean waters and strong tidal flows, it supports a mix of marine habitats and is used by small fishing vessels.
