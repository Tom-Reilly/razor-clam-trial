Gigha Summary Sheet

The Isle of Gigha (Trial Area 8) lies just off the west coast of the Kintyre Peninsula. Its waters are relatively calm, with sandy patches and seagrass beds supporting a rich coastal ecosystem. Gigha’s sheltered inshore grounds are regularly used by small fishing vessels and aquaculture operations.
