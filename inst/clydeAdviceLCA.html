Length Cohort Analysis (LCA)

Length Cohort Analysis (LCA) is an assessment method that uses commercial catch composition data (length frequency data) and estimates of growth parameters and natural mortality to estimate fishing mortality at length. The length based assessment approach applied here is consistent practice for data limited fisheries and is supported by fishery dependent data collected in collaboration with industry.

FMAX estimated by the LCA model is used as a proxy for FMSY in this assessment. Although FMSY proxies were estimated in the previous assessment, due to the changes in parameters (Blackman et al., 2025) and updated landings data the FMAX (and therefore FMSY) were re-estimated during this assessment and current fishing levels (Fbar) were compared to the updated FMSY level rather than the previously estimated value.
