Firth of Clyde Summary Sheet

The Firth of Clyde (Trial Area 9) is one of Scotland's largest sea inlets, stretching from Arran and the Kintyre coast up toward the central belt. Historically important for fishing, the area includes a mix of deep channels, sheltered lochs, and sandy inshore grounds. It remains an active maritime and recreational region.
