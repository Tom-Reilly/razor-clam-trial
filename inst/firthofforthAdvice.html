Firth of Forth Summary Sheet

The Firth of Forth (Trial Area 11) is a major Scottish estuary flowing east from Stirling to the North Sea. The area combines natural coastal habitats with busy ports and urban shorelines. Its inshore sandy grounds support a range of shellfish species, while the wider firth remains a key marine and transport corridor.
