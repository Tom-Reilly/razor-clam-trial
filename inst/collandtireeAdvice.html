Coll and Tiree Summary Sheet

Located in the Inner Hebrides, the islands of Coll and Tiree (Trial Area 6) sit to the west of Mull and are open to Atlantic waters. The surrounding seabed includes sandy and mixed sediment grounds that host diverse marine life. The area is remote, exposed, but is fished for razors when the weather allows.
