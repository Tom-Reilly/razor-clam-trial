Wigtown Bay Summary Sheet

Wigtown Bay (trial area 10) is part of the Solway Firth and forms one of the UK’s largest local nature reserves. Its shallow, sandy and muddy habitats support extensive shellfish beds and rich birdlife. The bay is known for strong tides and traditional inshore fishing activity.
