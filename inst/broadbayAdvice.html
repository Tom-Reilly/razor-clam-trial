Broad Bay Summary Sheet

Broad Bay (Trial Area 1) lies on the east coast of Lewis in the Outer Hebrides. It is a large, open bay with a sandy seabed and relatively sheltered waters. The area is influenced by Atlantic conditions but is popular for targeting shellfish.
