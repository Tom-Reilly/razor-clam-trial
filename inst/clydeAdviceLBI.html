Length Based Indicators (LBI)

Length Based Indicators (LBI) are empirical measures, derived directly from the size composition of the catch. Further details on sampling of razor clams can be found on the Data Collection page. Fishing often removes the largest individuals first, so stocks under high fishing pressure tend to contain fewer large animals and more small ones. By comparing measured sizes against biological reference points, the results can be displayed using a traffic light system to support fisheries management decisions. Work was undertaken as part of a PhD study in collaboration with the Scottish Association for Marine Science, University of the Highlands and Islands, University of Stirling, and Marine Directorate of the Scottish Government. Further details and outputs from these analyses are available at this link to the PhD thesis.
