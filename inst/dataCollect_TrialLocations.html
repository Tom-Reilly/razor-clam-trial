Trial Locations

Eleven trial areas (grouped into four zones) were established based on the consultation process, engagement with fishers and wider stakeholders including representatives from processing and export businesses. These eleven areas were further refined to consider restrictions due to the Scottish Marine Protected Areas (MPA) network, the presence of suitably classified waters for harvesting by Food Standard Scotland (FSS), and advice from Nature Scotland regarding Habitats Regulations Assessments and the potential impacts or mitigations for protected features. Fishing for razor clams is only permitted in the classified shellfish harvesting areas which are published on a weekly basis. Trial area 4 (Sound of Barra) and the Luce Bay area of Trial Area 10 are not currently part of the trial.
